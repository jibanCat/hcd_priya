VERDICT (numbers above):
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - the resolution correction itself is ~+5.8% in P (low-z high-k);
    i.e. the LF is ~-5.5% deficient there (matches the
    prior diag_lf_vs_hr_highk ~-6%).
  - inter-sim CoV(rho) low-z high-k median ~0.6% : the raw theta(+alpha)-spread of the correction across the 6 sims (SMALL).
  - LOSO: WORST per-sim coherent low-z high-k residual ~+0.91%, per-sim RMS up to ~1.5%.
    Compare to: correction effect ~|5.8|%, and the n_s-bias level ~1% logP.
  -> WORST per-sim coherent residual < 1% (the n_s level) and RMS well below
     the correction: over THIS 6-sim sub-volume the THETA-INDEPENDENT
     correction GENERALIZES well enough that the FIXED gbar does NOT itself
     re-introduce a coherent n_s-level bias. MF does not merely reshuffle it.

  COVERAGE CAVEAT (critical): the 6 HR sims span ns in [0.859, 0.979] only,
  Ap 1.29e-09-2.32e-09, clustered mid-high. This LOSO tests theta-insensitivity ONLY
  over that sub-volume. LOW-ns and the sparse ns>0.98 / >1.0 edge are UNTESTED.
  With 6 points this is a WEAK (necessary, not sufficient) test: leave-one-out
  over 6 clustered sims cannot certify theta-insensitivity globally; it can only
  show whether a fixed correction self-consistently predicts a held-out sim
  INSIDE this cluster. Extrapolation to the prior edges is not validated here.
