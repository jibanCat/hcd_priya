--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
(3) EXCESS TEMPLATE R_c = P_c - P_clean : its LF->HR ratio (P_HR exc/P_LF exc)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  This is what enters the forward model P_obs = P_clean + Sum_c alpha_c R_c.
  KEY: the 'excess' is SIGNED and SIGN-STABLE per class --
    LLS    : R_c > 0 (adds a little power at low k, ~0 at high k)
    subDLA : R_c < 0 (SUPPRESSES power; ~-15% of clean)
    DLA    : R_c < 0 (strongly suppresses; ~-50% of clean)
  The ratio rho_excess = R_HR/R_LF is well-defined where R_LF and R_HR share
  sign AND |R_LF/P_clean| is sizeable (>2%); near R_c~0 the ratio is unstable.

  LLS    : excess/clean (low-z hik median) = +0.7% of clean P (SIGN +)
           rho_excess (well-defined cells): all-band mean=0.978  low-z hik mean=0.823
           rho_clean low-z hik = 1.068  -> |rho_excess-rho_clean| band-mean = 26.63pp
           (70% of finite (z,k) cells have a sizeable, sign-stable excess => ratio well-defined)
  subDLA : excess/clean (low-z hik median) = -14.5% of clean P (SIGN -)
           rho_excess (well-defined cells): all-band mean=0.986  low-z hik mean=1.093
           rho_clean low-z hik = 1.068  -> |rho_excess-rho_clean| band-mean = 3.74pp
           (100% of finite (z,k) cells have a sizeable, sign-stable excess => ratio well-defined)
  DLA    : excess/clean (low-z hik median) = -49.9% of clean P (SIGN -)
           rho_excess (well-defined cells): all-band mean=0.965  low-z hik mean=1.065
           rho_clean low-z hik = 1.068  -> |rho_excess-rho_clean| band-mean = 0.93pp
           (100% of finite (z,k) cells have a sizeable, sign-stable excess => ratio well-defined)

  INTERPRETATION: where the excess is SIZEABLE (subDLA/DLA: large negative,
  ~60-100% of cells well-defined), rho_excess ~ rho_clean -> the clean rho
  applied to the whole P_obs (clean + signed excess) resolution-corrects the
  excess CORRECTLY. The LLS excess is tiny (<1% of clean) and sign-changes
  toward high k, so its ratio is ill-conditioned -- but a tiny excess carries
  negligible weight in P_obs, so a clean rho mis-correcting it is harmless.

  for DLA (fewest counts) the per-class signal may be at or below the 6-sim

  statistics CANNOT resolve a real per-class difference -- 'class-independent'
  there means 'not distinguishable', not 'proven identical'.
