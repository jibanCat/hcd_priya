## 2. Implementation scrutiny (per the charge)

### (a) Diagnostic scripts — no fakery found

- **jacrev / β (`diag_ns_slope_attenuation.py`).** The truth slope is a per-(z,τ₀-cell) multivariate OLS
  of logP_clean on the 9 unit-cube params (column NS_I+1 = ∂logP/∂ns at fixed others) — the correct
  partial. The emu slope is `jacrev(log predict_P_filt[clean], argnums=0)[:,NS_I]` — the matching object.
  β is computed three ways (median, mean, divide-free regression slope through origin) and they agree
  (0.98). The p40 |slope_true| mask correctly guards the n_s pivot (slope≈0 at k≈0.005) where a ratio
  would blow up. The shape-corr sanity gate (+0.997) confirms the autodiff slope is the right object
  BEFORE β is read. **The script even argues AGAINST its own original hypothesis** (β≈1 ⇒ NOT attenuation)
  — this is the opposite of a script tuned to confirm a prior, and it is what redirected the investigation
  to the data/KS leg. Credible.

- **per-row→per-z g-grouping (`diag_nsbias_z_attribution.py`).** The decomposition is the exact linear
  Fisher identity: `g = Σ_rows Jl[row]ᵀ·(C⁻¹dP)[row]`, `grow = Jl·w[:,None]`, rows partitioned by nearest
  global z-bin via `grow[m].sum(0)`, and `bias_z = (g_z @ Cpost[:,NS_I])/σ_ns`. Because `dx = Cpost@g` is
  linear in g and g = Σ_z g_z exactly, Σ_z bias_z = total IDENTICALLY — and the gate confirms it to 2e-13.
  There is no double-counting (each kept row lands in exactly one zbin via `argmin|zg−z|`), and the
  DESI/KS sub-split is the same partition by leg name. **Correctly coded.**

- **C_emu toggle.** `cemu_mode∈{prod, data_only, inflate2}` switches ONLY the C_total construction
  (`szb/rzb→None` for data_only; `cemu_inflate×2` for inflate2) — the Jacobian `Jl`, the residual `dP`,
  and the prior `Pp` are untouched, so the toggle isolates the covariance's effect on the MAP shift
  cleanly. The result (±0.01σ) correctly licenses the conclusion "the −0.65σ is not a C_emu-sizing
  artifact." **Correct.**

- **LF↔HR matching (`diag_lf_vs_hr_highk.py` / `diag_mf_rescorr_loso.py`).** Match key is
  `(params_unit rounded 6, z rounded 4, alpha_idx)` — identical to `multifidelity.match_hr_to_lf`. The
  alpha_idx axis is the τ₀ rung and `alpha_slope` is bit-identical per idx across caches (the comment
  claims it; my reproduction with 2060 exact matches and 6 sims is consistent with an exact, not
  nearest, match). HR is log-log interpolated DOWN onto the LF grid (HR k_max≫LF k_max ⇒ interpolation,
  never extrapolation) — the correct direction. I reproduced the headline (−7.59% at z=2.0, tilt-shaped)
  with my OWN matching loop, so the matching is not hiding the effect. **Correct.**

**No bug that would fake any conclusion.** The scripts are unusually careful (gates inside the per-sim
loop AND pooled; the kscut scan even avoids importing `diag_emu_bias_allfolds.py` top-level so it does not
truncate the authoritative anchor `.txt`).

### (b) z_lo=2.4 change + golden — clean

- The change is a one-line default + docstring in `load_ks_leg` (`z_lo=2.4`). The cut is applied via the
  SAME `keep` boolean mask that slices both P and the covariance block (`_assemble_leg` uses `np.ix_`), so
  the covariance ordering stays consistent. DESI is untouched.
- **Golden regeneration is legitimate.** I extracted the committed (HEAD) golden and diffed:
  `DESI_P` and `DESI_C` are **byte-identical** (`np.array_equal == True`) — the forward physics is
  unchanged, exactly as required. KS shrank 84→72 rows (12 = z=2.0,2.2 × 6 k-bins). So the only golden
  change is the KS *shape*, which is the intended effect of the cut.
- **One nuance I verified (not a defect, but worth recording):** the retained KS rows are NOT byte-identical
  to the old golden's z≥2.4 block — they differ by ~7e-4 (rel) in P, ~8e-5 in C. Root cause: the golden's
  `dla_core` is the leg z-MEAN (`jnp.mean(ctx.dla_core_leg[leg], axis=0)`), and dropping the two low-z
  bins changes that z-mean, which feeds every retained row. This is the production path being faithfully
  re-frozen (correct), but it means the golden no longer guards "per-z forward is byte-stable on retained
  rows" — it guards the new config. The invariant that matters (DESI byte-identical, forward unchanged) IS
  held. 20 tests pass in the two affected files; 47 across data_likelihood+multifidelity. The "29 pass"
  count in the handoff is the data_likelihood module's own count after the T4e cross-class additions; the
  number is plausible and the relevant subsets all pass.

### (c) MF plan (z+τ₀-resolved resolution correction) — feasible, three risks

The plan wants to replace the α-pooled `gbar(z,k)` with a `gbar(z,τ₀,k)` table. Reading
`multifidelity.py`:

- **Mechanically feasible.** `FixedMeanHead` already z-interpolates `gbar_tab` via `jnp.interp(z_phys,
  z_tab, col)` and is fully differentiable. τ₀ is ALREADY in the conditioning vector (`make_cond` appends
  it as `cond[10]`; `FixedMeanHead.__call__` simply doesn't read it yet). The repo ALSO already contains a
  differentiable bilinear-on-a-per-row-grid interpolator — `interp_res_corr` — which is exactly the
  template for a `(z,τ₀)` bilinear `gbar` lookup (searchsorted bracket + lerp in each axis, clamped edges).
  So the building block exists; the work is to (i) make `fixed_mean_table` bin by (z, alpha_idx/τ₀) instead
  of pooling alpha, and (ii) have the head do a 2-D clamped-bilinear lookup on (z_phys, τ₀). This is a
  contained change to two functions, no new architecture.
- **Differentiable for NUTS.** A clamped-bilinear lookup is C0-continuous and differentiable a.e. (the same
  smoothness `interp_res_corr` and the z-interp already rely on). The new τ₀-gradient path is benign and
  desired (the correction should respond to the sampled τ₀). The frozen-LF `stop_gradient` discipline is
  untouched (gbar is a fixed host-side table, not a trainable leaf).
- **JAX traps it avoids / must keep avoiding:** the module already documents the `eqx.filter_jit` (not
  `jax.jit(mf.P_mf)`) trap, the static-vs-dynamic `lf_model` freeze trap, and filters the benign
  "JAX array set as static" warning. A τ₀-resolved `gbar_tab` is a dynamic leaf (like the current one) —
  fine. The ONE trap to watch: if the (z,τ₀) lookup uses `searchsorted` on a τ₀ grid whose spacing varies,
  keep it CLAMPED-edge (no NaN propagation) exactly as `interp_res_corr` does, or grads at the table edge
  go NaN.

**MF-plan assessment:** sound and feasible, with caveats below.

## 4. Concerns (severity + concrete fix)

### IMPORTANT-1 — z_lo=2.4 (the chosen default) was NEVER run all-folds; only z_lo=2.6 was.
The kscut scan's primary grid is `A_zlo = [2.0, 2.1, 2.3, 2.8]` (no 2.4). z_lo=2.4 appears ONLY in the
EXTENSION block and ONLY on the 4-fold SUBSET {0,2,5,7} (+0.041σ). The only ALL-FOLDS (60-sim)
confirmation of a closing cut is **z_lo=2.6 (+0.038σ)**, not the committed default 2.4. The subset z2.0
baseline (−0.775σ) also differs materially from the all-folds anchor (−0.646σ), so the subset is a biased
estimator of the all-folds number — which means the +0.041σ subset value for 2.4 is not a tight bound on
the all-folds 2.4 number.
*Fix:* run ONE all-folds (60-sim) config at z_lo=2.4 and record it next to the z_lo=2.6 all-folds number.
This is the exact default the production likelihood ships; it should have a 60-sim closure number, not a
30-sim subset one. (Cheap: one `run_config({"k_max":0.06,"z_lo":2.4}, folds=range(8))`.)

### IMPORTANT-2 — the MF τ₀-resolution will be fit from a 6-sim cache where τ₀ and z are tangled.
`diag_mf_rescorr_loso.py` TEST 1c shows the τ₀ effect is +4.3pp (rung0 vs rung19), comparable to the n_s
level — so a τ₀-resolved gbar is warranted. BUT the same script's TEST 1b shows ns has univariate R²=0.15
against rho across the 6 clustered sims, and the (z,τ₀) interaction has a documented sign-flip. With only
6 sims spanning ns∈[0.859,0.979], a `gbar(z,τ₀,k)` table risks aliasing a *cosmology* signal (especially
the n_s tilt it is meant to neutralize) into the τ₀ axis — re-introducing exactly the over-fit the
`delta_mode='mlp'` head was removed for (n_s Fisher bias 4σ → why `delta_mode='none'` is the default).
*Fix:* build the τ₀-resolved gbar as `log_rho(k) + gbar_z(z,k) + gbar_τ(τ₀,k)` (ADDITIVE, separable),
NOT a free 2-D `(z,τ₀)` table, so the τ₀ axis is a 1-D marginal correction that cannot absorb the (z,k)
tilt; and re-run `diag_mf_rescorr_loso` AND `diag_ns_slope_attenuation`-style n_s-Fisher-bias check on the
τ₀-resolved forward before adopting it. The (z,τ₀) sign-flip interaction the plan notes is the warning
sign that a full 2-D table will over-fit.

### IMPORTANT-3 — the small-scale-leg C_emu floor (~0.9% LOSO) must be sized OUT-of-sample and on the
right object. The +0.91% worst per-sim coherent residual is the LOSO (held-out) number — good — but it is
measured on the RAW CACHE clean-forest P1D, not through the actual MF forward (frozen LF backbone +
log-log tail extrapolation into the KODIAQ band). The forward's interp/extrap adds its own error above the
LF Nyquist (0.069 s/km), which the raw-cache LOSO explicitly does NOT probe (the script flags this). A
floor sized from the raw-cache LOSO may under-cover in the 0.07–0.2 band where the LF is a tail
extrapolant.
*Fix:* size the small-scale C_emu floor from a LOSO run THROUGH `MultiFidelity.logP_mf` (the production
forward, including the tail extrapolation), not from the raw-cache ratio — and report it separately for
the LF-resolvable band (k<0.069) vs the extrapolated KODIAQ band (k>0.07).

### MINOR-1 — the "86%" attribution figure is imprecise.
The KS z=2.0+z=2.2 contribution is −0.676σ (KS-only) and the KS leg total is −0.685σ = **106%** of the
−0.646σ total (DESI offsets +0.04σ). "86%" does not match any decomposition I can reconstruct from the
npz. The conclusion (low-z KS dominates) is unaffected.
*Fix:* state it as "KS z2.0+z2.2 = −0.68σ ≈ the entire −0.65σ; DESI is +0.04σ (clean)" — the numbers in
the committed `.txt` already say this; just align the handoff prose.

### MINOR-2 — the golden's `dla_core` z-mean coupling is undocumented.
The retained-KS-row drift (~7e-4) when low-z bins are dropped is benign but surprising for a "drop rows"
change; a future reader diffing the golden will not expect retained rows to move.
*Fix:* add one line to `make_legb_golden.py` / the z_lo docstring noting that the leg-mean `dla_core`
(hence every retained row's P_model) shifts slightly when the z-range changes — it is the production path,
not a regression.

## 5. zlo_assessment (2.4 vs the paper z<2.8)

**Sound, and 2.4 is the better choice than a literal 2.8 — but it needs the all-folds number (IMPORTANT-1).**

The z-attribution localizes the bias entirely to KS z=2.0 (−0.47σ) and z=2.2 (−0.20σ). z=2.4 contributes
+0.009σ (clean) and every z≥2.4 KS bin is in-gate. So the bias is GONE the instant z=2.0+2.2 are dropped —
the closing cut is z_lo≥2.4, and 2.4, 2.6, 2.8 all close equally well (subset: +0.041, +0.046, +0.049σ;
2.6 all-folds +0.038σ). The paper's z<2.8 is a *conservative* DLA-incompleteness cut; the data say the
emulator-vs-data closure only needs z<2.4. Keeping z=2.4 and 2.6 (rather than discarding them to z<2.8)
retains real KS constraining power at no closure cost — a defensible, evidence-led choice that is LESS
aggressive than the paper while still removing the contaminated bins. **The one gap: certify 2.4
all-folds.** I would not ship the default on a 30-sim subset number when the production likelihood uses
exactly z_lo=2.4. If the all-folds 2.4 number ever came back materially worse than 2.6 (no reason to
expect it from the attribution, but unverified), z_lo=2.6 is the safe fallback with an all-folds +0.038σ
already in hand.

## 6. mf_plan_assessment (z+τ₀-resolved MF correction; LOSO caveats; ns-edge)

**The z+τ₀-resolved correction is the right call and is mechanically feasible**, with the separability
guard in IMPORTANT-2. The repo already has the differentiable machinery (`interp_res_corr` bilinear
template; τ₀ already in `cond`; `FixedMeanHead` z-interp), so this is a contained change — extend
`fixed_mean_table` to bin by τ₀ and the head to a 2-D clamped lookup. **Do it ADDITIVELY/separably**
(`log_rho(k)+gbar_z(z,k)+gbar_τ(τ₀,k)`), not as a free 2-D `(z,τ₀,k)` table: with 6 clustered sims and a
documented (z,τ₀) sign-flip, a full 2-D table will alias n_s/cosmology into the τ₀ axis and re-create the
`mlp`-head over-fit (the reason `delta_mode='none'` is the validated default; n_s Fisher bias 4σ for the
learned head). **Re-run the n_s Fisher-bias check THROUGH the MF forward** (not raw cache) after wiring,
per the plan's step (b) — the diag_emu_bias_allfolds-through-MF run is the right cert and must be on the
production forward (LF backbone + tail extrapolation), since the raw-cache LOSO does not probe the
extrapolated KODIAQ band where the LF is unreliable.

**LOSO caveats:** the 6-sim LOSO is correctly labeled necessary-not-sufficient. Its +0.91% worst coherent
residual ≈ the n_s level justifies the C_emu floor, but the floor MUST be sized on the production forward
out-of-sample (IMPORTANT-3), and reported per-band (LF-resolvable vs extrapolated). The ns∈[0.859,0.979]
coverage means the τ₀-resolved gbar is *trained* on a sub-volume; its extrapolation to low-ns and the
sparse ns>0.98/>1.0 edge is unvalidated.

**ns-edge handling:** keeping the domain [0.8,1.05] with a step-inflated C_emu above ns=0.995 (PI decision)
is the right move and is consistent with the 2/60 sparse-extension reality. The MF gbar's τ₀-resolution
does NOT help the ns-edge (the 6 HR sims top out at ns=0.979); the step-inflated C_emu is the correct,
independent guard. I agree with the PI decision.
