# Provenance of `joss_architecture.png`

**What it is.** A schematic of the emulator: its inputs (the nine PRIYA parameters, the redshift, the mean flux), the
four class spectra, the incidence outputs (dN/dX and the CDDF) and the mixture of the classes. It holds no measured
or predicted number.

**Where it comes from.** Figure 1 of the manuscript on hcd_priya prepared for the Journal of Open Source Software
(Ho, Garg and Avestruz, in preparation). The file is the manuscript's `fig1_schematic.png`, unchanged:
sha256 `5a88f31f9d247d3c1f5b3f854a051fb8c54fed728d8edc4d7ecafdfdab074c22`, 173,659 bytes. The manuscript's
repository is not public until submission; the figure is published here first by decision of the project lead
(2026-11-20).

**How it was made.** By a matplotlib script (`fig1_schematic.py` of the manuscript's repository), from shapes and
text only: no image, logo or artwork of a third party is used. The font is DejaVu Sans, which matplotlib ships and
whose licence permits this use. The labels follow the public documentation of this repository.

**Approval.** Approved by the project lead for the manuscript on 2026-11-12 and, in the form of the file first
published here, on 2026-11-19; approved for this tutorial on 2026-11-20. That file was replaced on 2026-11-25 by the
manuscript's current figure, on the project lead's word: it has one more label, a capital Theta on the arrow that
leaves the encoder.

journal's licence (CC BY 4.0).
